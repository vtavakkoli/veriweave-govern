# Support

## Community support

Use GitHub Issues for reproducible defects and narrowly scoped feature
requests. Before opening an issue:

- search existing issues;
- reproduce against the latest version;
- remove secrets and sensitive organizational data;
- include the relevant version, configuration, logs, and expected behavior.

Public support is best-effort and does not include guaranteed response times,
architecture review, production incident response, or compliance advice.

## Security support

Suspected vulnerabilities must be reported privately according to
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Commercial support

Commercial support may include production deployment guidance, prioritized
maintenance, integration design, policy-model review, private fixes, and agreed
response targets. See [`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) or
contact `vahid.tavakkoli@aau.at`.

## Scope limitations

VeriWeave Govern does not provide legal advice, regulatory certification, or a
guarantee that a policy configuration is complete or correct. Organizations
remain responsible for policy approval, risk assessment, human oversight,
security architecture, and operational authorization.
